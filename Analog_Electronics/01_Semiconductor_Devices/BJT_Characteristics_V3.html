关键公式

\[I_C = \beta I_B \qquad I_E = I_B + I_C = (1+\beta)I_B\]
\[\alpha = \frac{\beta}{1+\beta} \qquad I_C = I_S e^{V_{BE}/V_T} \left(1 + \frac{V_{CE}}{V_A}\right) \qquad g_m = \frac{I_C}{V_T},\ V_T \approx 26\,\text{mV}\]
