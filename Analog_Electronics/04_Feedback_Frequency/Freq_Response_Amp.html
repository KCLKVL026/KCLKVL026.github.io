关键公式

\[g_m=\frac{A_M}{R_L'},\qquad r_\pi=\frac{\beta_{BJT}}{g_m},\qquad I_C=g_mV_T,\qquad C_M=C_\mu(1+A_M)\]
\[f_H\approx\frac{1}{2\pi(C_\pi+C_M)r_\pi},\qquad H(s)=-A_M\frac{s/\omega_L}{1+s/\omega_L}\frac{1}{1+s/\omega_H}\]
