Kirchhoff's Voltage Law (KVL)

\(\sum V = 0\) Around any closed loop, the algebraic sum of voltage rises equals the algebraic sum of voltage drops. Regardless of how component parameters change, KVL always holds strictly. This simulation demonstrates KVL verification for 3 loops: left loop (\(U_1\)-\(R_1\)-\(R_2\)-\(R_4\)), right loop (\(R_2\)-\(R_3\)), and outer loop (\(U_1\)-\(R_1\)-\(R_3\)-\(R_4\)).

Kirchhoff's Current Law (KCL)

\(\sum I = 0\) At any node, the total current flowing into the node equals the total current flowing out. In this circuit, at the top middle node B: \(I_{R1}\) (inflow) = \(I_{R2} + I_{R3}\) (outflow). KCL always holds exactly. Adjusting any resistor value verifies this relationship.
