Theoretical Basis

First-order RC high-pass filter, input \(V_{in}=1\angle 0^\circ\),
Transfer function: \(H(f) = \frac{j2\pi f RC}{1+j2\pi f RC}\).
Magnitude: \(|H(f)| = \frac{2\pi f RC}{\sqrt{1+(2\pi f RC)^2}}\).
Phase: \(\varphi(f) = 90° - \arctan(2\pi f RC)\) (leading).
Cutoff frequency \(f_c = \frac{1}{2\pi RC}\), where \(|H| = 0.707\), \(\varphi = +45°\).

Characteristic Interpretation

• High-pass characteristic: low-frequency signals are suppressed, high-frequency signals pass with minimal attenuation.
• Phase lead monotonically decreases from +90° (very low frequency) to 0° (very high frequency).
• Adjusting \(R\) or \(C\) changes the cutoff frequency; the curve updates in real time.
• Click "Mark Cutoff Frequency" to automatically position the purple marker line at \(f_c\).
