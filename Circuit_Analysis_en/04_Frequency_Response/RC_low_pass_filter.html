Theoretical Basis

First-order RC low-pass filter, input \(V_{in}=1\angle 0^\circ\),
Transfer function: \(H(f) = \frac{1}{1+j2\pi f RC}\).
Magnitude: \(|H(f)| = \frac{1}{\sqrt{1+(2\pi f RC)^2}}\).
Phase: \(\varphi(f) = -\arctan(2\pi f RC)\) (lagging).
Cutoff frequency \(f_c = \frac{1}{2\pi RC}\), where \(|H| = 0.707\), \(\varphi = -45°\).

Characteristic Interpretation

• Low-pass characteristic: low-frequency signals pass with minimal attenuation, high-frequency signals are heavily attenuated.
• Phase lag monotonically varies from 0° to -90°, with a maximum lag of 90°.
• Adjusting \(R\) or \(C\) changes the cutoff frequency; the curve updates in real time.
• Click "Mark Cutoff Frequency" to automatically position the purple marker line at \(f_c\), synchronized across both magnitude and phase charts.
