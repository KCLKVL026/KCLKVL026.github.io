Theoretical Basis

Series RLC circuit, output voltage taken across the resistor:
\(H(s) = \dfrac{sRC}{s^2 LC + sRC + 1}\).
Center frequency \(f_0 = \dfrac{1}{2\pi\sqrt{LC}}\), where gain is maximum (1V).
Quality factor \(Q = \dfrac{1}{R}\sqrt{\dfrac{L}{C}}\), bandwidth \(BW = f_0 / Q\).
Phase: \(\varphi(f) = 90° - \arctan\left(\dfrac{2\pi f RC}{1 - (2\pi f)^2 LC}\right)\).
Passband boundaries (-3dB points): \(f_L = f_0\left(\sqrt{1+\frac{1}{4Q^2}}-\frac{1}{2Q}\right),\ f_H = f_0\left(\sqrt{1+\frac{1}{4Q^2}}+\frac{1}{2Q}\right)\).

Characteristic Interpretation

• Band-pass characteristic: signals pass only near the center frequency; both high and low frequencies are attenuated.
• Phase smoothly transitions from +90° (low frequency) to -90° (high frequency), with 0° at the center frequency.
• Adjusting \(R, L, C\) changes the center frequency and bandwidth; higher \(Q\) means sharper selectivity.
