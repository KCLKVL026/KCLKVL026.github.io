Theoretical Basis

Series RLC circuit, output taken as the sum of inductor and capacitor voltages (total voltage minus resistor voltage):
\(H(s) = \dfrac{sL + 1/(sC)}{R + sL + 1/(sC)} = \dfrac{s^2 LC + 1}{s^2 LC + sRC + 1}\).
Notch frequency \(f_0 = \dfrac{1}{2\pi\sqrt{LC}}\), where gain is minimum.
Quality factor \(Q = \dfrac{1}{R}\sqrt{\dfrac{L}{C}}\), higher \(Q\) produces a sharper notch.
Passband boundaries (cutoff frequencies) are the frequency points where gain drops to \(1/\sqrt{2}\) (\(-3dB\)) of maximum gain.

Characteristic Interpretation

• Band-stop characteristic: signals near the notch frequency are strongly attenuated, while passband signals on both sides pass through.
• Phase undergoes a rapid jump at the notch frequency, with the range of change related to the \(Q\) value.
• Adjusting \(R, L, C\) changes the notch center frequency and notch width.
